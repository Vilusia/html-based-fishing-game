Game Guide

Getting Started

Movement

W
ASD

Use WASD to move around the world.

Press SPACE to jump over obstacles.

Camera

Right-Click + Drag to rotate the camera

Press C to toggle first-person view

Press H to hide the HUD for screenshots

Hotbar

Press 1-6 to switch between tools:

1: Rod 2: Hands 3: Axe 4: Guitar 5: Glove 6: Pickaxe

Fishing Guide

How to Cast

1. Equip your fishing rod (press 1)

2. Hold Left-Click to charge your cast

3. Release to throw the line

4. Wait for a fish to bite!

Reeling Minigame

When a fish bites, a minigame appears:

🐟 ▬▬▬

Hold Left-Click to move the capture bar UP

Release to let it fall DOWN

Keep the fish inside the green zone to fill the progress bar!

Bait

Visit the Bait Shop NPC to buy better bait!

Better bait = Higher chance of rare fish!

Some bait unlocks exclusive fish species.

Fishing Zones

Lake - Central area, common fish

Dark Pond - Night creatures, rare spawns

Light Pond - Sun-loving species

Ocean - Deep sea fish (access via dock)

Lava World - Volcanic fish (special access)

Skills & Gathering

Lumberjacking (Axe)

1. Equip the Axe (press 3)

2. Find a tree

3. Left-Click near the tree to chop

4. Collect Wood to sell at the Merchant!

Oak Willow Maple Yew Magic

Higher skill = Better wood types!

Mining (Pickaxe)

1. Equip the Pickaxe (press 6)

2. Find ore rocks

3. Left-Click near the rock to mine

4. Collect Ore to sell at the Merchant!

Copper Tin Iron Gold Mithril Adamant Runite

Higher skill = Access to rarer ores!

Floristry (Collecting)

Find flowers scattered around the world.

Walk up to a flower and refer to the prompt:

"PRESS E TO COLLECT"

Flowers give Instant Money and Floristry XP!

Daisy (Start) Dandelion (Lvl 10) Tulip (Lvl 20) Rose (Lvl 30)

New types unlock every 10 levels!

Viewing Your Skills

Click the Skills button in the top bar to see your levels!

Skills use an XP system - keep gathering to level up!

Exploration

Day & Night Cycle

Daytime - Brighter, some fish only appear now

Nighttime - Darker, rare nocturnal fish spawn

The world has a full day/night cycle!

Weather

Sometimes it rains! During rain:

• Sky gets darker

• Rain-exclusive fish can be caught!

Void Portals

Mysterious portals randomly appear!

Walk into one to enter The Void

Catch rare Void Fish only found there!

Portals disappear after 2 minutes!

Lava World

Find the Inferno Guard NPC (lava icon)

He guards the entrance to the Lava World!

Catch fire-themed fish in molten lakes!

Ghost Cat

A mysterious black cat sometimes appears...

Approach it if you dare!

Multiplayer & Social

Voice Chat

Hold V to talk to nearby players!

Voice is proximity-based - get closer to hear better!

Text Chat

Press Enter to open chat

Type your message and press Enter again to send

Chat bubbles appear above your head!

Guitar

1. Press 4 or G to equip guitar

2. Click Load MIDI to upload a .mid file

3. Other players hear your music!

Boxing Glove

Press 5 to equip the glove

Left-Click to punch nearby players!

It's all in good fun!

Props

Press I to open the Props menu

Place items like campfires, tents, and more!

Press O to undo your last prop

Meow!

Press Z to meow at other players!

Progression

Making Money

Find the $ Merchant NPC near the lake

Press E to interact and sell:

• Fish from your inventory

• Wood from lumberjacking

• Ore from mining

Customization

Find the Stylist NPC (stylist icon)

Buy and customize:

• Hats, Shirts, Patterns

• Fur colors, Eye colors

• Different species!

Fishing Journal

Click Journal in the top bar

Track every fish species you've caught!

Fish have different tiers: Normal → Glistening → Shining → Opalescent

Can you catch them all?

Rod Upgrades

Better rods give bonuses:

• Longer cast distance

• Better catch rates

Check the Stylist for rod cosmetics!
