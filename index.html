Patch Notes

New features, improvements, and fixes for HTML Fishing.

October 30, 2026 — Chat Image Protection

Latest update · v12.519

  • 3D body paint: paint directly on your 3D character in the Body tab, including the head, torso front/back/sides, both arms and both legs, and pick any custom brush color. A whole-body Mirror toggle keeps matching marks on both halves, arms, legs and head sides. Outfits and hair are removed while Paint mode is active and restored when it is turned off or the studio closes. Paint is saved with outfits and included in saved characters and multiplayer sync.
  • Shareable outfits: copy a saved outfit code from the wardrobe or the Looks tab and import it on another character. Imported looks are validated, save with your wardrobe, and only equip clothing you own.
  • High-ping recovery: after a connection stall, queued movement, boat controls and other normal packets can arrive in a burst without triggering the packet-flood kick. Sustained spam, oversized messages and invalid traffic are still blocked.
  • Background-tab voice: voice proximity now follows incoming network positions even while the game tab is inactive, so players who walk into range are audible again. Returning to the tab also resumes any suspended audio. Mute and house isolation still apply.
  • Quest-style tutorial: the walkthrough is a right-side quest list showing the current chapter's steps and a clear Do this instruction. It measures the tournament HUD every tick, so anchor ads or an expanded tournament can no longer make the card overlap it. Text-only steps appear in a centered card, then dismiss and continue automatically. Objectives check off and advance without a Next button; action progress is recorded before the tracker initializes or while paused. Full instructions and chapter jumping remain under the ? button.
  • Paint mode guidance: the Character studio now explains body painting with numbered steps, a clear active/paused status, Fine/Medium/Wide brushes, one-click Undo stroke and a confirmation before Clear all.
  • Enhanced lighting: the world now uses filmic tone mapping with a warmer sky bounce, a cool side fill, cleaner shadow edges and a stronger moon. Sunrises and sunsets blend through warm sunlight, while rain, lava and Void areas keep their own balanced lighting. This applies whether the retro shader is on or off.
  • Real rain: falling drops are now thin, wind-slanted streaks that follow you instead of square sprites. The procedural rain sound layers a soft filtered body with fine high-frequency hiss for fuller rainfall.
  • Cleaner menus: Lucky Lou's scratch shop now has a Close button inside the panel instead of the floating red X. Escape now closes the top open menu, including scratch cards, shops, inventory, options, dialogs, trades, the screen queue and other overlays.
  • Retro shader: Display now renders the world through a chunky pixel shader with richer color, a soft vignette and light grain. It is on by default for new and existing saves, and an explicit OFF choice is remembered. Pick 2×, 3× or 4× pixels and toggle it at any time. The lower internal resolution can also improve performance.
  • Live server analytics: the master server now serves a live connections dashboard at /analytics, showing current players, active lobbies, host types and live sessions, plus weekly, monthly and yearly concurrent-player graphs. History starts with the first sample after the updated server starts.
  • Bigger scenery catalog: the level editor gained 17 new buildings and 37 new props, including cottages, taverns, chapels, barns, a forge, bakery, greenhouse, farmhouse, gazebo, ruins, treehouse, fishing shack, boathouse and pier, plus pine/palm/dead trees, boulders, lily pads, cattails, picnics, swings, wells, fountains, statues, wagons, canoes, buoys, fishing racks, crab traps, anvils, firewood and market goods.
  • Editor rotation and ear fix: R / Shift+R now rotates buildings, props, NPCs, trees, rocks and caves by 45°. Inner ears now use one clean inset surface instead of separate shrunken facet triangles, removing the pink cross/seam patterns on pointed and round ears.
  • Default island refresh: rebuilt the home island's look with one central lake, a glowing light pond, a dark pond, the cave fishing grotto, village paths and lanterns, a Void rift, a lava shrine, and the shared generated archipelago offshore. All NPCs, the boat dock, and the player house sit along the new routes, with clearer shores, reeds, lilies, rocks, and garden details. Benches now sit off the walking paths and face the ponds, house and dock. Clearing a custom island now reloads this refreshed default map with fresh scripts instead of an older cached island.
  • Pattern shop retired: the old fur-pattern category and its purchases are gone; free body paint replaces them.
  • Clearer sitting motion: increased seated breathing and hand movements so they are easier to see at the normal camera distance, with hand adjustments occurring more often.
  • Relaxed sitting animation: seated players now breathe gently and occasionally shift their hands, including boat passengers. Legs stay planted and held items keep their grip.
  • Complete NPC HUD hiding: Hide HUD now hides every NPC name and overhead icon, including Captain Finn, Morrow, the Inferno Guard and custom-map NPCs. Labels created while the HUD is hidden stay hidden, and return when the HUD is shown.
  • Shop hover try-on: hover or keyboard-focus hats, tops, pants, collars and titles to see a temporary preview on your character. Moving away restores your equipped look; previewing never purchases or equips an item.
  • 3D shop previews: hats, shirts, pants and collars now show the actual 3D character in shop cards, using one shared cached thumbnail renderer for fast category switching. The old 2D player previews, their fallbacks and the obsolete 2D Models toggle are gone, so character previews are 3D only.
  • True 3D studio thumbnails: style cards now render the actual character models, including current colors, clothes, hair, extras and protogen signs. Head features use a closer view and tails use a rear view. Shared rendering, caching and incremental loading keep large categories responsive.
  • New character studio: create your look with a large rotatable preview, icon-led categories, visual style cards, species starting points, color palettes, search, undo/redo and quick camera views. Browse owned clothing and save or load favorite looks. The layout adapts to phones, and protogen sign drawing stays available beside Extras.
  • Custom protogen signs: selecting Side Circles & Fins in Extras reveals a neatly placed Draw sign button. Draw, erase, undo or clear a sign with mouse or touch, then save it to both side circles in your eye color. Signs are saved with your character/outfits and visible to other players.
  • Restart Twitch at live: added Restart at live (this device) to open a fresh live-channel player instead of resuming your paused or rewound position. Only your player restarts; the room queue and other viewers stay unchanged. Playback starts automatically where permitted, with one muted retry if Twitch blocks sound.
  • Twitch playback recovery: removed pause and channel-reset commands from same-channel live requests, leaving playing viewers uninterrupted. Renamed the control to Resume live for room and added a larger Play here button for devices that require a local click. The menu now clarifies that Twitch live delays cannot be precisely synchronized.
  • Approved chat image hosts: inline images now require direct HTTPS links from Imgur, Reddit image CDNs or the supported GIPHY media hosts. Other URLs remain plain text without an automatic image request. Added image-source browser restrictions to block redirects to other hosts and disabled referrer sharing on chat/GIF images.

October 29, 2026 — Exploration & Gameplay Update

Update · v12.480

  • Lobby ownership protection: public listing updates and removal now require a private host capability, preventing other users from replacing an existing lobby’s join target. Includes safer listing registration, expiry recovery and server request limits. Requires the updated master directory service; room-code joins remain available during the upgrade.
  • Twitch resync without player reloads: live catch-up now uses Twitch player controls and keeps the existing player on outdoor screens and house TVs. Sound opt-in and proximity muting also use player controls when available, avoiding reloads. Initial playback can still require a tap; exact live timing remains limited by Twitch and buffering.
  • Twitch control loading fix: corrected the screen script cache version so updated browsers load the Resync to live button in the stream queue, including house TVs.
  • Twitch live resync: screen owners can use Resync to live in the stream queue to reload the live channel for everyone, including house TV viewers. Keeps the queue and individual sound preferences; buffering can still vary between viewers.
  • Ear and protogen update: inner ears now follow their outer surfaces instead of floating in front. Protogen ears are taller and swept back. Replaced the Extras visor with paired side circles and triangular fins; their inner screens match your eye color. The face visor remains available under Nose.
  • Interactive tutorial: a full chapter-based walkthrough now covers movement, fishing, every NPC, shops, gathering, cooking, exploration, homes, boats, social tools, events and custom maps alongside the written guide. Follow direction arrows and complete real objectives, with mobile instructions, saved progress, pause/resume and optional task skipping.
  • Stream Player display fix: corrected the rebuilt outdoor screen housing to face the video overlay, preventing its backing from hiding Twitch streams or YouTube videos.
  • Cleaner lobby setup: lobby creation now matches the server browser with one unified panel, aligned privacy and age options, a collapsible tag picker, compact selected-tag badges, and a clear-tags action.
  • Nearby footsteps only: footsteps now fade out within a shorter range, and distant movement sounds are rejected before playback. Remote sounds use the sender’s tracked position, stay within the same house or outdoor space, and no longer replay your own sounds through the host.
  • One placed home per player: the Props menu now allows one home at a time, including pending placements. Undo your home before moving it; undo also works in solo play. The host enforces the same limit for guests.
  • Room spam safeguards: all client message handlers now share packet-rate and size limits. Sustained flooding or repeated malformed packets trigger an automatic kick, prop cleanup, and a two-minute reconnect cooldown. Placed props are capped at 10 per player and 100 per room, outdoor screens retain a five-screen cap, and duplicate IDs and distant placements are rejected. Shared video queues are capped at 50 entries, rooms allow at most 32 guest connections, and a failed peer send no longer interrupts broadcasts to others.
  • Shared exploration finds: search randomly placed coin pouches, drift bottles, and treasure caches for money on the default home island, custom-map land, and offshore islands. Offshore finds pay more. The first collector earns the reward and the find disappears for everyone; new finds appear every 15 minutes. Rewards retry until saved, and late joiners receive the current shared finds.
  • Custom-map island shores: generated islands on custom levels now have broad sandy beaches, greener, flatter interiors, and irregular coastlines instead of dome-shaped hills. Default island terrain keeps its existing shape.
  • 3D personal rowboat: the Misc tool shop rowboat now uses a hollow wooden 3D hull with benches and paired oars, including a matching shop preview. Oars row while moving and settle when stopped, for you and other players in both character modes. Replaced the rowing hiss with softer water pulls and bubbling splashes timed to the strokes.
  • Animated prop fires: campfires and tiki torches now have swaying, flickering flames and light. Lanterns have a flickering flame inside their glass, and BBQ grills have glowing coals and small dancing flames beneath the grate.
  • Reliable tournament results: guests request fresh results when the timer expires or updates stall. Hosts retry prizes until the recipient confirms saving them, and saved prize receipts prevent duplicate payments after reloading.
  • Outdoor prop overhaul: rebuilt all 26 outdoor decoration models with a coordinated low-poly style and added a leafy bush. The Props menu now sells permanent unlocks using game money; purchased props can be placed repeatedly for free. Updated seat surfaces to fit the new models and batched outdoor models for more efficient rendering. House furniture and house placement keep their existing systems.
  • Creation credit: the main menu now notes that the game was created with AI assistance.
  • Fitted inner ears: corrected pointed, round, and floppy 3D ear interiors so they follow the outer ear surfaces and tilt without sticking out.
  • Protogen visor corners: fixed body-colored corners showing on the 3D face screen; the screen now stays dark all the way to its edges.
  • Protogen face screen: the Protogen nose now forms a large dark visor with screen eyes and a talking mouth in your selected eye color, in both character renderers.
  • Matching blue main menu: updated the main menu panels, buttons, text, borders, and lake illustration to match the blue palette used throughout the other game menus.

How to compete

Solo players and groups of any size can participate. There are no minimum-player or minimum-catch requirements. Catch 10 fish to earn $50; catch 50 to earn $250. With at least 3 participants who caught a valid fish, 1st place earns an extra $5,000, 2nd $2,500, and 3rd $1,000. With fewer participants, everyone receives only $5 per fish. Ties go to the first player to reach the score.

New rounds start every hour on the host's clock. Catch a fish to enter automatically. Each valid fish counts once; boots do not count. Ties go to the player who reached that score first.

Your cast and catch must finish in the same round. The host checks known fish, duplicate catches, and cast timing. Prizes are awarded automatically when the round ends.

Tournaments belong to this lobby and reset when the host closes it. Disconnected winners can collect pending prizes by returning with the same peer ID while the host session stays open.
